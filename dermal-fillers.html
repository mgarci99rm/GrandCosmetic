The Process

What to Expect

We start by discussing the look you're going for and the area you'd like to enhance. A numbing cream is usually applied first for comfort. The filler is then carefully injected to add volume and shape, with results visible immediately — any swelling settles within a few days. Results typically last 6–18 months depending on the area and product.

Dermal Fillers is a prescription treatment. Candidacy is determined by a licensed provider at your consultation. Individual results vary, and timing and duration differ from patient to patient.
