How Semaglutide Works: The Science Behind the Medication

Semaglutide is a synthetic version of a natural peptide called GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1), which is produced in the intestines to help regulate blood sugar levels. It triggers insulin release and prevents excessive glucagon secretion, leading to better blood sugar control and reduced hunger. By mimicking the action of GLP-1, semaglutide reduces appetite and increases feelings of fullness, which results in reduced food intake and steady weight loss. This powerful mechanism of action helps individuals achieve their weight-loss goals without invasive procedures or drastic lifestyle changes.
