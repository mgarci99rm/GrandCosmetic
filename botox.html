The Process

What to Expect

Your appointment starts with a short consultation to map your expression lines and talk through your goals. The injections themselves take just a few minutes, with a fine needle and minimal discomfort — most patients describe it as a quick pinch. Most patients return to their normal activities right after. Results build gradually over 3–7 days and typically last 3–4 months.

Botox is a prescription treatment. Candidacy is determined by a licensed provider at your consultation. Individual results vary, and timing and duration differ from patient to patient.
