01

Botox

A neuromodulator that gently relaxes the muscles behind expression lines.

Helps with: forehead lines, frown lines (11s), and crow's feet, for a smoother, more rested look.

02

Dysport

A fast-acting alternative to Botox, known for a soft, natural spread.

Helps with: the same expression lines as Botox, with results some patients notice sooner.

03

Jeuveau

A modern neuromodulator formulated specifically for aesthetic use.

Helps with: moderate to severe frown lines, for a refreshed, natural expression.

04

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic-acid based gels that restore volume and definition.

Helps with: lips, cheeks, under-eyes, chin, jawline, nasolabial folds, marionette lines, neck, temples, hands and nose.

05

Hylenex

An enzyme treatment used to dissolve or adjust previously placed filler.

Helps with: correcting asymmetry, migration, or simply changing your mind.

06

Kybella

An injectable treatment that permanently destroys fat cells under the chin.

Helps with: reducing submental fullness ("double chin") without surgery.

Before You Book

House Rules

Deposit

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required to reserve your appointment. It's applied toward your service and is valid for 30 days from your original appointment date. We accept cash and major credit cards (no American Express or personal checks).

Cancellations

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. If you rebook beyond 30 days, a new deposit is required.

Arrivals & No-Shows

Arrive on time — late arrivals may lose treatment time, with full payment still due. No-shows forfeit their deposit in full.
