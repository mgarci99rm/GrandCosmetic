The People Behind Your Care

Meet the Team

Dr. Behrang Dehkordi, MD

Medical Director

A board-certified sports medicine physician specializing in non-surgical orthopedics. A former college soccer player, he earned his MD from the American University of the Caribbean and completed his residency at Hennepin County Medical Center, followed by a fellowship at the University of Illinois at Chicago. He has served as team physician for multiple universities and professional soccer teams, and remains active in soccer and skiing.

Tricia Santos, NP

Nurse Practitioner

Born in New York and raised in the Philippines, Tricia earned her Bachelor's Degree in Nursing in 2014 and her Master's Degree in Nursing in 2021. She specializes in non-surgical aesthetic treatments — neuromodulators, dermal fillers, medical weight loss and laser procedures — and takes pride in helping patients look and feel their best.

Before You Book

House Rules

Deposit

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required to reserve your appointment. It's applied toward your service and is valid for 30 days from your original appointment date. We accept cash and major credit cards (no American Express or personal checks).

Cancellations

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. If you rebook beyond 30 days, a new deposit is required.

Arrivals & No-Shows

Arrive on time — late arrivals may lose treatment time, with full payment still due. No-shows forfeit their deposit in full.
