The Process

What to Expect

Your appointment begins with a skin consultation, then the peel solution is applied in layers. The treatment takes about 20–30 minutes with minimal discomfort. Plan for about 7 days of downtime: peeling usually starts around day 3, lasts 2–4 days, and is generally finished by day 7. Results keep improving over several weeks.

Candidacy is determined by a licensed provider at your consultation. Individual results vary, and timing and duration differ from patient to patient.
