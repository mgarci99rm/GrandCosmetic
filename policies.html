Appointment Policy

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required at the time of reservation. Your deposit will be applied toward your service at the time of your appointment.

As a courtesy, we require at least 24 hours' notice for any appointment changes or cancellations.

Deposits are valid for 30 days from the original appointment date. If you choose to reschedule after the 30-day period, a new non-refundable $50 deposit will be required to secure your appointment. Both the original and new deposits will be credited toward your service and deducted from the total cost of your treatment.

Please keep in mind, your appointment time is reserved especially for you; not showing leaves your injector with an empty chair. Late arrivals may have time subtracted from their services, but will be responsible for full payment. While we will make every effort to perform your entire treatment in the remaining scheduled time, we reserve the right to reschedule your appointment if we feel there is not enough time to give you quality treatment and not keep other clients waiting. No Shows will result in forfeiting your deposit completely.

During botulinum toxin appointments, our Nurse Practitioners assess the facial muscles and decide what number of units are sufficient for adequate treatment. This can vary from patient to patient and from treatment to treatment. To provide the best level of patient care, we recommend you follow up with an appointment or call 14 days after your botulinum toxin appointment if further treatment is needed. If additional units are needed, we will require additional payment.

Refunds

Treatment services are non-refundable and non-transferable. We do not offer refunds on services rendered. All injectable treatment sales are final; refunds or credits cannot be offered once treatment is completed. We would offer to fix any issues instead.
