Sermorelin therapy is suitable for adults experiencing common signs of aging, including fatigue, reduced muscle mass, disrupted sleep, and weight gain. Sermorelin Anti-Aging: FAQs

What is the difference between Sermorelin and HGH therapy for anti-aging?

Sermorelin stimulates the pituitary gland to naturally produce growth hormone, which helps maintain stable and consistent hormone levels in the body with minimal fluctuations. On the other hand, HGH therapy directly provides synthetic growth hormone, resulting in an immediate boost in hormone levels.

Is Sermorelin therapy safe?

Under the supervision of a qualified medical professional, Sermorelin therapy is considered safe. However, a comprehensive health evaluation is essential to ensure that the therapy is suitable for your individual health needs and goals.

How often are Sermorelin injections administered?

The frequency of Sermorelin injections is customized based on individual health requirements and wellness objectives. After starting therapy, follow-up appointments will assess progress and allow adjustments to injection frequency and dosage as needed.
